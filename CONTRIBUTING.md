# Contributing to GRC workspace

Issues and pull requests are welcome.

This project (package `grc-workspace`) is licensed under the MIT License. By submitting a contribution, you agree that it may be included under that license.

Do not submit official NCA, SAMA, or PDPL control text. The app uses original workspace labels, not copies of official control wording.

Do not commit secrets, `data/`, `.env`, or `session.key`.
